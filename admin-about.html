About FeedSync

FeedSync is a centralized platform used to collect and manage academic feedback across ICCT Colleges – Cainta Campus. It improves administrative decision-making by providing organized insights on facilities, departments, administrative services, and student support systems. The system ensures that feedback is accessible, evaluated properly, and used to support a more responsive and student-centered school environment.
